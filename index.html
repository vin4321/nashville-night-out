    How the score works

    Score out of 100tap to read

    Every place gets the same formula; “The details & score” on each card shows its parts.

    • Reviews, up to 60: Google and Tripadvisor ratings (OpenTable where that's all there is), averaged by number of reviews. Places with few reviews are pulled toward a typical 4.3★, so ten 5-star reviews can't beat thousands of 4.7s. 3.6★ scores 0 and 4.8★ scores 60.
    • Critics, up to 25: Michelin star 4 points, Bib Gourmand 3, Michelin Recommended 2, James Beard 2, each critic list 1 (Eater, The Infatuation, NYT, Bon Appétit), capped at 6.
    • AI lists, up to 10: how many of the three original AI-made lists named it.
    • Research check, 5: held up as a pick in the Oct 20 review of recent reviews and closures.
    • Reviews flag, −10: a cited critic ranks it near the bottom (shown on the card).
    • “Top pick” = top quarter by score in each tab. Ratings read from Wanderlog and OpenTable on Oct 21, 2026.

    What reviewers flag

    Popular spots not in this guidetap to read
    • Jimmy Buffett's Margaritaville (Lower Broadway): #15 of 17. The Infatuation warns that whatever you order “is a one-way ticket to a five-alarm hangover.” Source
    • Redneck Riviera (Lower Broadway): Last of the 17: “a divey joint with no stage to speak of,” on a street where the stage is the point. Source
    • Loveless Cafe (Highway 100, well west of downtown): Famous biscuits, but reviewers quoted by Islands were told lunch would take about three hours, and one called it “not worth 2½ hour wait.” Source
    • Bartaco (Several Nashville locations): No reservations, and one reviewer quoted by Islands waited close to two hours for a table. Hard with six. Source
    • Late nights on Lower Broadway (Broadway): Reviewers quoted by Islands call it “overrun with loud, rude, drunk people,” and a local says they no longer feel safe there after dark. Do Broadway early, then finish in Printers Alley by the hotel. Source
    • Driving and parking downtown (Downtown): Parking near Lower Broadway can run $40–50 or more. Most of this guide is walkable from the Dream; ride-share the rest. At the airport, check the taxi line: one traveler was quoted $63 for an Uber, against about $30 by cab. Source

    Places were checked on their own websites in October 2026. Walking and driving times are routed on real streets from the Dream (OpenStreetMap / OSRM); walks up to 15 minutes show as walks, longer hops as drives without traffic. Directions open Google Maps from wherever you are.
